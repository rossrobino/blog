## Barriers to create

Computers and technology has historically been a relatively inaccessible field to enter.

1. Price - good computers cost $
2. Vision largely required to view the display and review work
3. Hands required to navigate interfaces and type

Various assistive technologies have been utilized such as VoiceOver or JAWS to assist visually impaired creators.

AI is the largest breakthrough in a variety of fields across all industries, specifically in software development it has broken a variety of barriers to entry.

## Good ideas required

Digital products have historically been hard to create. Now the cost and time to deliver has been effectively eliminated, good ideas are few and far between.
